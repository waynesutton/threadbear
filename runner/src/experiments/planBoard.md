## What it does

Ask a thread for a plan ("Plan a live sales dashboard for Acme on a board") and its agent draws it with `update_board`: boxes, dashed groups, labelled arrows and a short note. The thread header gets a **Show plan** button, and the transcript a "Drew a plan (v1)" line. Show plan opens the board as a tab beside the thread: pan with two fingers, pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom, drag boxes where you like (they stay put across new versions; Tidy lays it out again). Click a box, group or arrow to put it in the message box as a chip, then say what to change; the agent writes a new version and its note says what changed. Nothing can be sent until you press **Approve plan**, which approves that version. Then a box's **Send to…** shows the brief it will send, the login (Claude or Codex), the access and the board's skills, and **Start** begins one nested thread for it. Each sent box shows its thread's state (Working, Needs you, Done, Failed, Archived, Can't see it), and Needs you also reaches the Inbox, notifications and the Dock badge the usual way. If the agent changes the plan after you approved it, every box's Send to… greys out until you approve the new version; boxes already sent keep running. A box finishing doesn't wake the planner: when every sent box is Done, **Ask planner to review** sends it one message, if you press it. A thread whose agent never drew a plan looks exactly as before.

## Lives in

The runner: `runner/src/boards.ts` (the only Mermaid parser, the files under `<dataDir>/boards/<threadId>/`, approval, sends, the brief, `BoardTools`, `boardText`, `withBriefHeader`), `runner/src/board-methods.ts` (`board.get`, `board.version`, `board.approve`, `board.move`, `board.skills`, `board.send`, wired in `runner/src/main.ts` with `boards`, `boardTools` and `boardsFor`), `runner/src/runner/boards.ts` (`emitBoard`, in runner.ts's parts), the `board.updated` event in `runner/src/protocol.ts`, and in `runner/src/threadbear-mcp.ts` the `read_board` and `update_board` tools, `create_thread`'s `board_node`, the `boards` option and `PLAN_BOARD_LINE` (also in main.ts's `richGuidance` for Codex). The contract is docs/protocol/events.md, "Plan boards". In the desktop app: the layout, plan state, brief, status mapping, chips and the Ask planner to review rule in `desktop/core/src/plan_board.rs`, the `BoardUpdated` event in `desktop/core/src/reducer/event.rs` (with its row in `model.rs` and `row_debug.rs`), the board tab in `desktop/gpui/src/plan_board.rs` (its panel id, title and view arms in `shell.rs`, the Show plan button in `header.rs`, the event hook in `app.rs`, and the `board` automation command in `automation.rs`). The spec is `features/plan-board.feature` with `features/steps/plan-board.steps.ts`, and the mock's `TB_MOCK_PLAN_BOARD` in `mock-runner/server.mjs` and `features/support/world.ts`. Tests: `runner/test/unit/boards.test.ts`, `runner/test/unit/board-methods.test.ts`.

## Taking it out

Delete this file, `runner/src/boards.ts`, `runner/src/board-methods.ts`, `runner/src/runner/boards.ts` (and `boardsPart`/`BoardsPart` in runner.ts), the `board.updated` event and its `PERSISTED_TYPES` entry in protocol.ts, `boards`, `boardTools`, `boardsFor`, `boardMethods` and `PLAN_BOARD_LINE` in main.ts, and in threadbear-mcp.ts the `boards` option and parameter, the two board tools, `board_node` with its block in `create_thread`, and `PLAN_BOARD_LINE`. Take out planBoardMockups and planBoardCoordinators first, as they need this. In the desktop app delete `desktop/core/src/plan_board.rs` and `desktop/gpui/src/plan_board.rs` with their `mod` lines, `BoardUpdated` in event.rs, model.rs and row_debug.rs, the plan board arms in shell.rs, the Show plan button in header.rs, the hook in app.rs and the `board` command in automation.rs. Then the "Plan boards" section of docs/protocol/events.md, the feature file and its steps, `TB_MOCK_PLAN_BOARD`, the two test files, and run `npm run experiment-docs`. Boards already on disk under `<dataDir>/boards` can be deleted or left; nothing else reads them.
